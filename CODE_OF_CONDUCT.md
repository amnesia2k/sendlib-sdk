# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, appearance, disability, nationality, ethnicity, religion, or other personal characteristic.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Accept feedback gracefully and explain technical decisions with evidence.
- Respect privacy, consent, and the sensitive nature of email infrastructure.
- Help keep discussions focused and accessible to contributors at different experience levels.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, stalking, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Insults, personal attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Sharing credentials, private email content, recipient data, or security details publicly.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to Olatilewa Olatoye at `tilewa.olatoyee@gmail.com`. Do not open a public issue containing private information. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and impartially as possible, with confidentiality respected to the extent practical.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other participation that violates this policy. Responses may include a private warning, temporary restriction, permanent removal from project spaces, or referral to the relevant platform or authorities when necessary. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern is prohibited.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing this community. Questions about SendLib employees, accounts, billing, or service conduct should go through SendLib's own support channels because this project is independent.
